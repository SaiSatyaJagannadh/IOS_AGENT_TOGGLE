## SWP — the operational rules [12]

The most detailed page in this cluster.

- **Withdraw 6–8%** to make the corpus last indefinitely
- **Do not run SWP from volatile funds.** Small-cap and similar fall too hard. Use **hybrid**
  categories, which recover faster after a fall
- **Move the corpus into debt/hybrid/balanced *before* starting the SWP**
- Keep a **3–5 year buffer** before withdrawals begin
- **Pause the SWP in a down market** — this is the sequence-of-returns defence
- **Step up the withdrawal 5–6%** — as the corpus shrinks, a flat withdrawal is a rising
  percentage and the tax profile worsens
