# Indian mutual funds

From *MF how to select by daytrader* [52] and *by money purse* [44], *Mutual funds money purse*
[42], *Hybrid/index* [43], *Small cap funds* [46], *Thematic funds* [41], *When to exit* [18].

## Horizon first

- **Debt** — under 1 year, or up to 5
- **Equity** — minimum 5–10 years. *"Don't fear about short term volatile."*
- SIP set up and left for decades is the notes' stated ideal

## Selection criteria

| Criterion | The note's rule |
|---|---|
| Plan type | **Direct + Growth** always. Never dividend — growth reinvests |
| Fund manager | Check their record; **frequent manager changes are a risk** |
| Standard deviation | Lower is better vs category |
| Expense ratio | Lower is better — **but** a higher-expense fund with better performance wins |
| Benchmark | Always compare against the index, not just peers |
| Sharpe etc. | Compare the technical ratios across candidates |
| Count | Hold few funds and actually compare them |

Tools: Value Research fund pages, AMFI (`amfiindia.com`) monthly data.

## Expense ratio mechanic worth knowing

The notes flag a common misconception: the expense ratio is **charged daily against NAV**, not
deducted on sale or once a year. Verify online — the note itself says "check online".

## Categories

- **Small cap** — 5-year minimum. **AUM > ₹10k cr probably won't deliver alpha.**
- **Hybrid** — conservative hybrid ≈ 80 debt / 20 equity; balanced 75/25 rebalanced through cycles
- **Arbitrage** — NSE/BSE spread capture, low risk (e.g. Tata Arbitrage)
- **Thematic** — only with cycle knowledge. Sectors named: Banking, IT, Pharma, Consumption.
  Prefer FoF for diversification; **invest lumpsum when the theme is down**, and check exit load
- **Index** — see `india-etf-index`; the notes generally prefer ETFs on cost

## Cycle awareness

Sector funds turn on rate cycles — *"IT sector will be low if Fed increases rates, vice versa."*
The notes cite Nifty Smallcap at −40% in FY20 followed by +118% in FY21, and use 10-year CAGR
(Nifty Midcap 16.3, Nifty 50 13.2) to set risk expectations.

## Debt funds [42]

- **Debt MFs hold a larger market than equity (27%)**
- Taxed at **your income slab** (not equity LTCG/STCG rules)
- Better than FD returns after tax for >5 years
- Rates up → bond value down, and vice versa
- Corporate bond funds ≈ AA- and above, ~80% stability; credit risk funds are the opposite end
- Idle cash for 1–2 months belongs here rather than savings — but weigh tax and expense ratio
- T-bills: 91 / 184 / 360 day

## Exit [18]

Review annually. Watch for exit load or charges creeping up quarterly, and check the underlying
sector's performance.
