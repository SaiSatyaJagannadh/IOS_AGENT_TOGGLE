# NRI transition checklist

From *NRI precautions India* [32], a 17-item checklist. Grouped by theme below; the source
numbering differs.

## Accounts and KYC

1. Convert savings account → **NRO** savings account
2. Open an **NRE** account for investment — makes money conversion easier
3. Check Aadhaar-related issues for KYC
4. Complete **NRI KYC** at a nearby office
5. Convert the **demat account** to NRI status

## Tax and professional help

6. **Visit a CA for taxation** — stated plainly, before the move
7. Farm land and property: ask the CA; taxation is heavier and specific laws apply

## Insurance — time-sensitive

8. Apply for an **NRI term policy while still resident**, allowing **~2 weeks** for medical exams.
   Check Policybazaar. Doing this after the move is harder

## What happens to existing holdings

| Instrument | Position |
|---|---|
| **EPF** | After 7 years it moves to senior citizen funds — **close it if possible** |
| **NPS Tier 1** | NRIs **can** invest. The note prefers it over MFs for the SWP option |
| **PPF** | Can continue if you opened it as a resident |
| **Sukanya Samriddhi** | Can continue **if the child is not an NRI** |
| **SGB** | **Cannot** invest |
| Stocks / MFs | Can be transferred to friends and family |
| Inheritance | **No restrictions** on inherited property or money |

Also: get USA documents in order [item 7 in the original].

## Note

This checklist reflects rules as captured and the page is undated. NRI taxation and eligibility
change often — and the note includes *"visit a CA for taxation"* as its own item, which
supersedes anything inferred from the rest.
