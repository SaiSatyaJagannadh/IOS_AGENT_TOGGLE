# The allocation order

From *Main investment* [19]. Short page, but it is the spine of everything else in these notes —
the order in which money is committed.

## The sequence

1. **Insurance first — health, then term.** Nothing else starts until this is in place
2. **ETFs**, bought **when they are down 5% or more**
3. **Mutual funds**, with **step-up** SIPs
4. **SWP plan**
5. **Stocks**
6. **NPS**
7. **Gold**

## The standing instruction

> **"Need to move money from high risk mfs to debt funds."**

A de-risking rule that sits outside the sequence and applies continuously.

## Why the order matters

Every other skill sits somewhere on this list, and the list says protection comes before returns.
A question like "should I buy this stock" is, by the note-taker's own ordering, premature if
health and term cover are not in place — see `india-insurance`.

The ETF entry rule (5% dip) is the same one in `india-etf-index`; the step-up mechanics are in
`india-mutual-funds` [36]; the SWP rules in `india-goal-planning` [12].
