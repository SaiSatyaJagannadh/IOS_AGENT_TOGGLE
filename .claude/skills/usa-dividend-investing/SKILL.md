## The sustainability test [26]

The one analytical rule here, and the most useful thing on the topic:

> **Check the coverage ratio** for dividend-paying stocks. It tells you whether the company can
> keep paying it in future, calculated from the balance sheet.

Yield alone is not a reason to buy. Coverage is what separates a payer from a future cut.
