## Futures mechanics

- Derivatives take their value from an underlying asset
- Three series: **near, next, far**
- **Primary stated purpose is hedging** — an offsetting position to reduce risk from adverse moves
- Expiry: **futures monthly on Thursday, options weekly**
- `25FEBFUT` = that month's Thursday February contract
- Spot price = live market price
- Sellers need not hold full cash upfront, but must fund **2–5 days before expiry** or the
  position is squared off
- Physical settlement means the seller must actually hold the shares
- Advantage cited: bulk exposure with less capital — which is leverage, and cuts both ways

Available on stocks, commodities, currency, indices.

## The directional rule

The notes' core decision, stated twice:

| Expected move | Action |
|---|---|
| **Greater than 1%** | **Buy** side — call buy |
| **Less than 1%** | **Sell** side — put sell |

Worked example: market at 33,100 with resistance at 33,280 → under 1% of headroom → put sell.

Conditions the notes require before taking the **sell** side: a move expected under 1%, global
markets confirming direction, **and** support/resistance in confluence. Call sell only for a
clear downside view.

**Thursday afternoon:** avoid the sell side — decay is already low into expiry.
