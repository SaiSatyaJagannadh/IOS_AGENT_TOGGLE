# Indian market basics

From *Stock market basics to depth* [47] — one of the largest pages in the notes — plus
*Investor psychology* [58] and *Copy cat investors* [57].

Opening line: **"Stock market is not a gambling and not easy."**

## Structure

- Depositories: **CDSL (BSE)** and **NSDL (NSE)** hold shares like banks hold money
- **BSE ~4,178+ stocks, NSE ~2,000+**; indices exist because most do not trade daily
- Main indices: **BSE Sensex 30**, **Nifty 50**
- Share types: **common** and **preferential** — preferential gets an edge on selling
- Investor types: **retail**, **DIIs** (MFs, insurers), **HNIs**, **FIIs/FDI**
- Caps: large cap = ranks 1–100 (the top 100 are blue chips), then mid, small, nano

## Formulas

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **EPS** | Total earnings in a year ÷ total outstanding shares |
| **PE** | Share price ÷ EPS |
| **Book value** | Total assets − total liabilities |
| **Book value per share** | Book value ÷ total outstanding shares |
| **PB** | Share price ÷ book value per share |
| **Face value** | Total equity share capital ÷ outstanding shares (the par value at issue) |
| **Share capital** | Face value × total number of shares |

**Dividends are declared on face value.** A ₹1 face value with a 1015% declaration is
**₹10.15 per share** — not a percentage of market price.

**When a dividend is paid the stock adjusts down** by that amount. **Bonus and splits give no
additional return** in themselves, though the company may grow over time.

## Macro to track

- **GDP** — low means weak economic activity and lower company values
- **Inflation** — announced monthly; **must be moderate**, neither high nor low. Watch **WPI** and **CPI**
- **IIP** — index of industrial production, per sector, monthly
- **Political stability** at the centre
- **RBI monetary policy** — rate cuts and new rules
- **Monsoon** — India-specific, via the meteorological department, because of food and agriculture

Macro dashboard: `tijorifinance.com/in/macro`.

## Behavioural biases, with the note's own examples

| Bias | The note's framing |
|---|---|
| **Anchoring** | Buying Nvidia on sight without checking fundamentals or future growth |
| **Action** | Checking the stock daily instead of following the company's news |
| **Loss aversion** | Fearing loss on what you don't invest — while inflation erodes you anyway |
| **Sunk cost** | Refusing to sell a loser hoping it recovers. Ask *why* it is falling; sell junk |
| **Herd mentality** | Assuming a large group must be right |
| **Social proofing** | Investing because a relative did |
| **Recency** | Chasing IPO listing gains for short-term profit |
| **Framing** | Marketing that leads with positives and buries negatives |
| **Self-attribution** | Profit is mine, loss is someone else's fault |
| **Outcome** | Copying a friend's trade with more money |
| **Overconfidence** | Quick gains followed by losses |

Reference given: **"The Art of Thinking Clearly."**

## Market mechanics

- **Buyback** signals a company doing well — but check; sometimes it is idle cash used to push the price
- **Circuits** — trading halts if the index falls 10% or 20%; rules vary, check current ones
- **Pre-market 09:07–09:08** (before the 09:15 open) sets the opening price
- **Shareholding pattern** — watch for **promoters and FIIs selling while retail buys**. Track the
  trend graph on Tijori
- E-voting runs through the **CDSL portal**

## Costly mistakes

- **Don't catch a falling knife**
- Watch for **mindless acquisitions** — buying up useless companies
- Never invest without knowing the company: balance sheet first
- **Don't hold 100+ stocks** — *"even Warren Buffett doesn't have a large portfolio"*.
  Analysing one company properly takes ~2 days. **15 stocks** is the working limit, fewer for
  someone with a job. *"If we don't have time to track stock then we are ducked."*
- **Diversify across sectors**, not within one
- **Don't chase multibaggers** — look for business growth, and multibaggers follow
- On a quality stock that drops, buy more; on monopolies, watch the rising peers
- **Trading is risky** — paper trade first, and prefer investing if you lack time
- *"If you do mistake in stock you have to pay penalty, not sorry to market"*
